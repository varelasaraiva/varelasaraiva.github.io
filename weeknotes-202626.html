  • I finished the week with one of those still-elusive nights where I got to sleep for six hours almost in one go. The following day, we met friends at IWM Duxford, an old WWII air base turned into a great air museum-one of my favourite places. Since the last time we were there, they've built a nice playground with a very sensible layout (yes, I'm looking at you, Bancroft Gardens in Hitchin). The children, of course, wanted to spend most of the day there. Thankfully, it was built right next to the airstrip, so that we could keep an eye on the Spitfires and Hurricanes taking off and landing. We were disappointed, though, that we didn't get to see the Corsair and the Mustang pictured below fly.
