This Saturday, we went to Hertford to meet the lovely baby E for the first time. It’s always nice to spend time in Hertford—it’s such a pleasant town.

* * *

On the digital gardening front, I finished a new feature: the photos page, where all photos posted in weeknotes are scraped and displayed in a mosaic of thumbnails. There were also some fixes to the RSS feeds to make them more standards-compliant.

* * *

I have been trying to get back to trading some of my phone screen time for my Kindle. Since last year, I’ve been trying several ways of having a healthier relationship with my phone: uninstalling social apps, installing a more boring, text-based, application launcher, blocking loading certain websites in the browser, etc.

Besides using my phone more intentionally, I’ve been trying to increase the reading throughput of actual books as well. Many people have adopted the ebook reader Xteink X4, which, with its small form factor, fits comfortably in a pocket and can go anywhere. It’s an interesting little device indeed. I may have large pockets but I think old 6” Kindles are mostly pocketable too so it should do the job.
